# Printer ink procurement (CISS printers, e.g. Epson L-series)

Domain notes for ink refills bought for the user's home CISS printers. The general marketplace procedure (seller verification, lazy-render, SKU checks) lives in SKILL.md.

## Series identification
- Look up printer model → ink series BEFORE searching. Epson L800/L805/L810 = **series T673** (6 colors: Black/C/M/Y/LC/LM, dye inks, original 70 ml bottles, C13T673xxx). Neighbor models use different series (L100/L200 = T664/T672) — wrong series wastes a full search cycle.
- **Original ink ships only in 70 ml.** Bulk 500/1000 ml for CISS models exists only as compatible (non-Epson) dye ink — that is the ONLY route for a user who wants big capacity, and it must be stated up front, not discovered mid-research.
- Compatible listings commonly mix series in one title ("664 672 673 674"). Confirm the exact series is present in the SKU before recommending (see marketplace step 4 in SKILL.md).
- Quality tiers: original = reference for photo printing; compatible = acceptable for CISS self-refilling but pick sellers with 95%+ positive feedback and real sales volume; unverified cheap sellers risk head clogging.

## Local (Israel) availability
- Israeli shops stock 70 ml bottles/kits only (original or ~6×70 ml compatible kits); big capacities = AliExpress compatible ink (2–4 weeks delivery, price ~4–10× cheaper per 100 ml).
